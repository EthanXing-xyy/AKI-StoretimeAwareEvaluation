# Reference Papers

## Primary study

**File:** `41746_2026_Article_2722.pdf`  
**Title:** *Deep learning models for acute kidney injury prediction: multi-center external validation and evaluation under simulated continuous monitoring conditions*  
**Authors:** Kyung Hyun Lee, Donghwee Yoon, Hyunsun Lim, Ki-Byung Lee, and Yong Kyu Lee  
**Journal and year:** *npj Digital Medicine*, 2026  
**DOI:** [10.1038/s41746-026-02722-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-02722-2)  
**License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

This is the primary study reproduced and extended by this repository. It provides the AKI prediction framework, five model architectures, 0/48/72-hour prediction horizons, simulated continuous-monitoring evaluation, and clinical-faithfulness and alert-burden analyses.

## Methodological reference

**File:** `jmir-2025-1-e73987.pdf`  
**Title:** *Challenges and Recommendations for Electronic Health Records Data Extraction and Preparation for Dynamic Prediction Modeling in Hospitalized Patients: Practical Guide and Tutorial*  
**Authors:** Elena Albu, Shan Gao, Pieter Stijnen, Frank E Rademakers, Bas CT van Bussel, Taya Collyer, Tina Hernandez-Boussard, Laure Wynants, and Ben Van Calster  
**Journal and year:** *Journal of Medical Internet Research*, 2025  
**DOI:** [10.2196/73987](https://doi.org/10.2196/73987)  
**License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)

This paper is the methodological reference for EHR data availability and temporal data preparation. It supports the project's motivation for evaluating EHR temporal availability, result availability, temporal leakage risk, and dynamic prediction data preparation. It does not define this project's AKI outcome or model architecture.

The PDF files are retained without modification. Each paper remains subject to its original publication license; the project-level MIT License does not apply to either PDF.
